# ODO NORD

**Business Management & Odoo Platform for Nord Holding Group**

Production ERP, portal, and operational modules powering the Nord Holding ecosystem.

## About

ODO Nord manages the Odoo-based business management platform for [Nord Holding AD](https://nordholding.bg) — a diversified Bulgarian holding company operating across real estate, agriculture, energy, and services.
